## Business Problem
How is the business performing overall, and where is that performance concentrated? This analysis examines overall sales performance and identifies where revenue is concentrated across products and regions to understand the key drivers of business performance.

## Stakeholder
Sales Manager. They need to understand overall revenue performance and identify which products and regions are driving sales, in order to guide resource allocation decisions.

## Decision
This analysis informs the specific decision of which regions and/or products should receive prioritized investment or marketing focus, based on where revenue and customer activity are concentrated.

## Unit of Analysis
Each row represents a single transaction, with details about the product, quantity, price, and customer information.

## KPIs
| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | KSH 3,206,450 |
| Number of Unique Customers | 113 |
| Average Revenue per Transaction | KSH 10,688.167 |

## Initial Findings
- Office Chairs generated the highest revenue of any product.
- Kisumu region far outperformed other regions in revenue generation.

## Recommendation
Prioritize sales resources in Office Chairs in Kisumu, which both generate the most revenue.

## Limitations
This analysis is descriptive, not causal. It identifies where revenue is concentrated, not why.
